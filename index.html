OUR HISTORY

Rooted in Faridabad
since 1991.

What began in a small rented home has grown into a trusted local source for paints, hardware and trade essentials—built over decades through customer relationships and a steadily expanding range.

The Kin Kin identity
THE BUSINESS TODAY

Built around materials,
not finished furniture.

Kin Kin serves customers looking for paint and coating products, wood-finishing materials, furniture-making fittings, workshop consumables and industrial hardware. The range includes varnish, melamine, lacquer, primers, handles, cutting wheels, racks, shelves and metal angles, along with selected garden and agricultural items.

The store is based in NIT-3, Faridabad, Haryana. Customers are welcome to enquire about product availability, sizes, specifications and pricing directly.

THE MILESTONES

Growing with
our customers.

1991

A humble beginning

Started from a storeroom in a rented one-bedroom home on a freehold property.

1993

Moved into our own shop

Moved into an owned shop and formally registered the business.

1994

A dependable source for locks

Customers increasingly came to regard the store as one of Faridabad’s dependable wholesale sources for locks.

1995

Paints added to the range

Expanded beyond hardware into paints.

1996

More industrial essentials

Added slotted angles and broadened the product range further.

1999

Furniture hardware expands

Added more items for furniture making and fitting.

2001

Fabrication supplies

Expanded into fabrication products and workshop consumables.

2021

Agriculture and gardening

Extended the range to include agricultural and gardening items.
