OUR HISTORY

A trading name
since 1993.

Kin Kin Paint & Hardware Store has been serving paint and hardware needs in Faridabad since 1993, as reflected in the store's own logo.

The Kin Kin identity
THE BUSINESS TODAY

Built around materials,
not finished furniture.

Kin Kin serves customers looking for paint and coating products, wood-finishing materials, furniture-making fittings, workshop consumables and industrial hardware. The range includes varnish, melamine, lacquer, primers, handles, cutting wheels, racks, shelves and metal angles, along with selected garden and agricultural items.

The store is based in NIT-3, Faridabad, Haryana. Customers are welcome to enquire about product availability, sizes, specifications and pricing directly.
